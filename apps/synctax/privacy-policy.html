Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026
Download PDF

This Privacy Policy explains how The DevMarq ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with SyncTax, our desktop software, website pages, and related webhook services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and licensing data such as your license key, hardware identifier, name, email address, and business contact details.
  • Configuration data you enter into SyncTax, including Meta access token, WhatsApp phone number ID, app secret, webhook verify token, webhook URL, and message template text.
  • Business data you choose to process through the app, such as spreadsheet rows containing client names, PAN numbers, phone numbers, and folder locations for PDF documents.
  • WhatsApp/Meta delivery metadata such as message IDs, status updates, phone number ID, and webhook timestamps.
  • Technical and security data such as device hardware ID, application logs, error logs, and timestamps.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • activate and verify licenses, and bind the license to a device;
  • send documents through WhatsApp Cloud API on your behalf;
  • receive and display message delivery statuses;
  • configure and verify your webhook connection;
  • match spreadsheet records to PDF documents and generate reports;
  • provide support, maintain security, and improve the product.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We share data only as needed to operate SyncTax and its integrations. This may include:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform, for messaging, webhook delivery, and account management;
  • Vercel, for hosting webhook endpoints and API routes;
  • Supabase, for license checks, client configuration, and temporary webhook status storage;
  • service providers that help us host, secure, and maintain the service.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. SyncTax is designed to keep webhook delivery events temporary; the desktop app purges per-client status events on close, and the server applies retention cleanup as a backup. License records may be retained while the license remains active and for legitimate business, security, and legal recordkeeping purposes.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us using the details below. You can also uninstall the application and delete the local app data folder to remove configuration stored on your device.

Children

SyncTax is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above shows the latest version.
