Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: May 29, 2026
Download PDF

SyncTax is designed to keep temporary webhook delivery events only as long as needed for operational use. You can request deletion of local and server-side SyncTax data by following the steps below.

1. Delete Local App Data

On Windows, remove the local app data folder for your user account:

  • %APPDATA%\SyncTax

On macOS or Linux, remove the corresponding per-user folder:

  • ~/SyncTax

This removes locally stored configuration such as license key, Meta settings, and app preferences.

2. Stop the Desktop App and Purge Server Events

SyncTax is designed to purge webhook delivery events for the active client when the app closes. If you want a manual server-side cleanup, send a deletion request by email using the subject line below.

3. Email a Deletion Request

Send your request to thedevmarq@gmail.com with the subject:

SyncTax Data Deletion Request

Include your license key, business name, and WhatsApp phone number ID so we can identify the correct records.

4. What Will Be Deleted

  • local configuration stored on your device, if you delete it;
  • temporary webhook status events associated with your configured WhatsApp phone number ID;
  • server-side client configuration, where deletion is permitted and operationally possible;
  • other SyncTax records that are not required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or billing purposes.

5. What May Be Retained

We may retain limited records required for accounting, legal compliance, fraud prevention, licensing, or security. Some Meta / WhatsApp records are also governed by Meta's own retention rules and can only be deleted through Meta's systems.

6. Confirmation

We aim to respond to deletion requests within a reasonable time and will confirm when deletion is completed or explain any retention required by law.
